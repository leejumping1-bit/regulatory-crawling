[조항 9] Safety and Effectiveness Requirements
[조항 10] 10 A medical device shall be designed and manufactured to be safe, and to this end the manufacturer shall, in particular, take reasonable measures to
[조항 10] (a)
[조항 10] identify the risks inherent in the device;
[조항 10] (b)
[조항 10] if the risks can be eliminated, eliminate them;
[조항 10] (c)
[조항 10] if the risks cannot be eliminated,
[조항 10] (i)
[조항 10] reduce the risks to the extent possible,
[조항 10] (ii)
[조항 10] provide for protection appropriate to those risks, including the provision of alarms, and
[조항 10] (iii)
[조항 10] provide, with the device, information relative to the risks that remain; and
[조항 10] (d)
[조항 10] minimize the hazard from potential failures during the projected useful life of the device.
[조항 10] SOR/2022-197, s. 12(F)
[조항 11] 11 (1)
[조항 11] A medical device other than a decorative contact lens shall not, when used for the medical conditions, purposes or uses for which it is manufactured, sold or represented, adversely affect the health or safety of a patient, user or other person, except to the extent that a possible adverse effect of the device constitutes an acceptable risk when weighed against the benefits to the patient and the risk is compatible with a high level of protection of health and safety.
[조항 11] (2)
[조항 11] A decorative contact lens shall not adversely affect the health or safety of a user, except to the extent that a possible adverse effect of the device constitutes a risk that is compatible with a high level of protection of health and safety.
[조항 11] SOR/2015-193, s. 2
[조항 12] 12 (1)
[조항 12] A medical device other than a decorative contact lens shall perform as intended by the manufacturer and shall be effective for the medical conditions, purposes and uses for which it is manufactured, sold or represented.
[조항 12] (2)
[조항 12] A decorative contact lens shall perform as intended by the manufacturer.
[조항 12] SOR/2015-193, s. 2
[조항 13] 13 During the projected useful life of a medical device, its characteristics and performance shall not deteriorate under normal use to such a degree that the health or safety of a patient, user or other person is adversely affected.
[조항 14] 14 The characteristics and performance of a medical device shall not be adversely affected by transport or conditions of storage, taking into account the manufacturer’s instructions and information for transport and storage.
[조항 15] 15 Reasonable measures shall be taken to ensure that every material used in the manufacture of a medical device shall be compatible with every other material with which it interacts and with material that may come into contact with it in normal use, and shall not pose any undue risk to a patient, user or other person.
[조항 16] 16 The design, manufacture and packaging of a medical device shall minimize any risk to a patient, user or other person from reasonably foreseeable hazards, including
[조항 16] (a)
[조항 16] flammability or explosion;
[조항 16] (b)
[조항 16] presence of a contaminant or chemical or microbial residue;
[조항 16] (c)
[조항 16] radiation;
[조항 16] (d)
[조항 16] electrical, mechanical or thermal hazards; and
[조항 16] (e)
[조항 16] fluid leaking from or entering into the device.
[조항 17] 17 A medical device that is to be sold in a sterile condition shall be manufactured and sterilized under appropriately controlled conditions, and the sterilization method used shall be validated.

[조항 78] Medical Devices for Investigational Testing Involving Human Subjects
[조항 78] Application
[조항 79] 79 This Part applies to medical devices that are to be imported or sold for investigational testing involving human subjects.
[조항 79] General
[조항 80] 80 (1)
[조항 80] Subject to subsections (2) and (3), no person shall import or sell a medical device for investigational testing.
[조항 80] (2)
[조항 80] A manufacturer or importer of a Class II, III or IV medical device may sell the device to a qualified investigator for the purpose of conducting investigational testing if the manufacturer or importer holds an authorization issued under subsection 83(1) and possesses records that contain all the information and documents required by section 81.
[조항 80] (3)
[조항 80] A manufacturer or importer of a Class I medical device may sell the device to a qualified investigator for the purpose of conducting investigational testing if the manufacturer or importer possesses records that contain all the information and documents required by section 81.
[조항 80] Records
[조항 81] 81 The records referred to in section 80 shall contain the following:
[조항 81] (a)
[조항 81] the name, address and telephone number of the manufacturer and the importer of the device;
[조항 81] (b)
[조항 81] the name of the device, its class and its identifier, including the identifier of any medical device that is part of a system, test kit, medical device group, medical device family or medical device group family;
[조항 81] (c)
[조항 81] a description of the device and of the materials used in its manufacture and packaging;
[조항 81] (d)
[조항 81] a description of the features of the device that permit it to be used for the medical conditions, purposes and uses for which it is manufactured, sold or represented;
[조항 81] (e)
[조항 81] a list of the countries other than Canada where the device has been sold, the total number of units sold in those countries, and a summary of any reported problems with the device and any recalls of the device in those countries;
[조항 81] (f)
[조항 81] a risk assessment comprising an analysis and evaluation of the risks, and the risk reduction measures adopted for the purposes of conducting investigational testing of the device, including, as appropriate,
[조항 81] (i)
[조항 81] the results of any previous research, testing and studies conducted with respect to the device,
[조항 81] (ii)
[조항 81] a description of the methods currently used to diagnose or treat the medical condition in respect of which the investigational testing is being proposed, and
[조항 81] (iii)
[조항 81] information respecting any cautions, warnings, contra-indications and possible adverse effects associated with the use of the device;
[조항 81] (g)
[조항 81] the names of all the qualified investigators to whom the device is proposed to be sold and their qualifications, including their training and experience;
[조항 81] (h)
[조항 81] the name and address of each institution at which the investigational testing is proposed to be conducted and, in the case of a Class III or IV device, written approval from the institution indicating that the investigational testing may be carried out there;
[조항 81] (i)
[조항 81] a protocol of the proposed investigational testing, including the number of units of the device proposed to be used for the testing, the hypothesis for and objective of the testing, the period of time during which the testing will be carried out and a copy of the patient consent form;
[조항 81] (j)
[조항 81] a copy of the device label; and
[조항 81] (k)
[조항 81] a written undertaking from each qualified investigator to
[조항 81] (i)
[조항 81] conduct the investigational testing in accordance with the protocol provided by the manufacturer,
[조항 81] (ii)
[조항 81] inform a patient who is to be diagnosed or treated using the device of any risks and benefits associated with its use, and obtain the patient’s written consent for its use,
[조항 81] (iii)
[조항 81] not use the device or permit it to be used for any purpose other than the investigational testing specified in the protocol,
[조항 81] (iv)
[조항 81] not permit the device to be used by any other person except under the direction of the qualified investigator, and
[조항 81] (v)
[조항 81] in the event of an incident involving the device and that meets the following conditions, report the incident and the circumstances surrounding it to the Minister and to the manufacturer or importer of the device within 72 hours after the qualified investigator becomes aware of the incident:
[조항 81] (A)
[조항 81] the incident is related to a failure of the device or a deterioration in its effectiveness or any inadequacy in its labelling or in its directions for use, and
[조항 81] (B)
[조항 81] the incident has led to the death or a serious deterioration in the state of health of a patient, user or other person or could do so were the incident to recur.

[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Medical Devices other than in Vitro Diagnostic Devices
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Invasive Devices
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 1
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (1)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Subject to subrules (2) and (3), all surgically invasive devices are classified as Class II.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (2)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A surgically invasive device that is intended to diagnose, monitor, control or correct a defect of the central cardiovascular system or the central nervous system or of a fetus in utero is classified as Class IV.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (3)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A surgically invasive device that is intended to be absorbed by the body, or that is normally intended to remain in the body for at least 30 consecutive days, is classified as Class III.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 2
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (1)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Subject to subrules (2) to (4), all invasive devices that penetrate the body through a body orifice or that come into contact with the surface of the eye are classified as Class II.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (2)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to be placed in the oral or nasal cavities as far as the pharynx or in the ear canal up to the ear drum is classified as Class I.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (3)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A device described in subrule (1) that is normally intended to remain in the body or in contact with the surface of the eye for at least 30 consecutive days is classified as Class III.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (4)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to be represented as preventing the transmission of infectious agents during sexual activities or reducing the risk thereof is classified as Class III.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 3
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Despite rules 1 and 2
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (a)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] all denture materials and orthodontic appliances, and their accessories, are classified as Class II;
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (b)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] all surgical or dental instruments are classified as Class I; and
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (c)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] all latex condoms are classified as Class II.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Non-invasive Devices
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 4
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (1)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Subject to subrule (2), all non-invasive devices that are intended to come into contact with injured skin are classified as Class II.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (2)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to be used as a mechanical barrier, for compression or for absorption of exudations, is classified as Class I.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 5
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A non-invasive device intended for channelling or storing gases, liquids, tissues or body fluids for the purpose of introduction into the body by means of infusion or other means of administration is classified as Class II.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 6
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (1)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Subject to subrules (2) and (3), a non-invasive device intended for modifying the biological or chemical composition of blood or other body fluids, or liquids, for the purpose of introduction into the body by means of infusion or other means of administration is classified as Class III.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (2)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A device described in subrule (1) whose characteristics are such that the modification process may introduce a foreign substance into the body that is potentially hazardous, taking into account the nature and quantity of the substance, is classified as Class IV.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (3)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A device described in subrule (1) that accomplishes the modification by centrifugation, gravity filtration or the exchange of gas or heat is classified as Class II.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 7
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (1)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Subject to subrule (2), all other non-invasive devices are classified as Class I.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (2)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] A device described in subrule (1) is classified as Class II if it is intended
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (a)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] to act as a calibrator, tester or quality control support to another medical device; or
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (b)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] to be connected to an active device that is classified as Class II, III or IV.
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Active Devices
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Rule 8
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] :
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] (1)
[조항 SCHEDULE 1] Subject to subrules (2) and (3), an active device intended to emit ionizing radiation, including any device or software intended to control or monitor such a device or directly influence its performance, is classified as Class III.

[조항 2] Use with respect to Transmissible Agents
[조항 2] Rule 1
[조항 2] :
[조항 2] An IVDD that is intended to be used to detect the presence of, or exposure to, a transmissible agent in blood, blood components, blood derivatives, tissues or organs to assess their suitability for transfusion or transplantation is classified as Class IV.
[조항 2] Rule 2
[조항 2] :
[조항 2] An IVDD that is intended to be used to detect the presence of, or exposure to, a transmissible agent is classified as Class II, unless
[조항 2] (a)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used to detect the presence of, or exposure to, a transmissible agent that causes a life-threatening disease if there is a risk of propagation in the Canadian population, in which case it is classified as Class IV; or
[조항 2] (b)
[조항 2] it falls into one of the following categories, in which case it is classified as Class III:
[조항 2] (i)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used to detect the presence of, or exposure to, a transmissible agent that causes a serious disease where there is a risk of propagation in the Canadian population,
[조항 2] (ii)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used to detect the presence of, or exposure to, a sexually transmitted agent,
[조항 2] (iii)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used to detect the presence of an infectious agent in cerebrospinal fluid or blood, or
[조항 2] (iv)
[조항 2] there is a risk that an erroneous result would cause death or severe disability to the individual being tested, or to the individual’s offspring.
[조항 2] Rule 3
[조항 2] :
[조항 2] An IVDD that is intended to be used for patient management is classified as Class II, unless it falls into one of the following categories, in which case it is classified as Class III:
[조항 2] (a)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used for the management of patients suffering from a life-threatening disease; or
[조항 2] (b)
[조항 2] there is a risk that an erroneous result will lead to a patient management decision resulting in an imminent life-threatening situation for the patient.
[조항 2] Other Uses
[조항 2] Rule 4
[조항 2] :
[조항 2] An IVDD that is not subject to rules 1 to 3 and that is intended to be used in diagnosis or patient management is classified as Class II, unless it falls into one of the following categories, in which case it is classified as Class III:
[조항 2] (a)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used in screening for or in the diagnosis of cancer;
[조항 2] (b)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used for genetic testing;
[조항 2] (c)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used in screening for congenital disorders in the fetus;
[조항 2] (d)
[조항 2] there is a risk that an erroneous diagnostic result would cause death or severe disability to the patient being tested or to that patient’s offspring;
[조항 2] (e)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used for disease staging; or
[조항 2] (f)
[조항 2] it is intended to be used to monitor levels of drugs, substances or biological components, if there is a risk that an erroneous result will lead to a patient management decision resulting in an imminent life-threatening situation for the patient.
[조항 2] Rule 5
[조항 2] :
[조항 2] An IVDD that is intended to be used for blood grouping or tissue typing to ensure the immunological compatibility of blood, blood components, tissue or organs that are intended for transfusion or transplantation is classified as Class III.
